# Dignified Days: Operational Workflow & Customer Service Optimization

## Executive Summary
Analysis of operational bottlenecks for Dignified Days, a multi-state provider of rental medical equipment for home hospice care[cite: 1].

## Core Operational Bottlenecks[cite: 1]
1. **Delivery & Pickup Inquiries:** Excessive call volumes from clients seeking schedule status despite tracking portal availability[cite: 1].
2. **Equipment Return Scheduling:** High CSR handling times coordinating reverse logistics[cite: 1].
3. **Malfunction Triage & Triage Deflection:** First-touch CSR transfer bottlenecks due to basic user error vs. genuine hardware faults[cite: 1].

## Proposed System Architecture & MOV
- Measurable Organizational Value (MOV) targets for call-volume deflection and automated scheduling.
- Tiered self-service intake workflows and dispatch scheduling integration.
